Sailing is more than a hobby. It shaped who I am. During my gap year with Ocean College I spent six months at sea on an Atlantic circuit, Ireland to France by way of Morocco, Cape Verde, the Caribbean, Central America and the Azores, mentoring 26 students along the way.
